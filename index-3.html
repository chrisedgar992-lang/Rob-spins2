Paytable & rules

Lines. 20 fixed paylines pay left to right from reel 1. Line pays shown are at your current bet.

Max bet. Bets go from 10 up to 2,000. Once your balance reaches 20,000 the max bet unlocks to 20% of your balance, rising every 10,000 (20k unlocks 4,000, 30k unlocks 6,000 and so on). If your balance drops, the max drops with it.

Rob Trains, the pink tank engine, is wild. It lands on reels 2, 3 and 4 and stands in for every symbol except the Chooks badge.

Chooks badge pays anywhere. 3, 4 or 5 award 2×, 10× or 50× your bet plus 10, 12 or 15 Chooks Spins.

Chooks Spins. The hooter sounds and the crowd's up. All wins pay double and every Rob Trains that pulls in stays in the station. 3 more badges is a try, worth 5 more spins.

Ea-nasir's Copper Stall. Ea-nasir on reels 1, 3 and 5 opens his stall. Open crates of his "fine" copper for 1× to 15× your bet until one of the 2 customer complaints shuts him down.

All Aboard. On any hand, Rob Trains can steam across the reels and leave 3 to 6 extra wilds on reels 2 to 5. Roughly 1 hand in 80.

Rob With the Pearl Earring. On any hand, Rob may glance over his shoulder from the canvas. Wherever his gaze lands, 3 to 6 symbols on reels 2 to 5 become Pearl wilds. Roughly 1 hand in 80.

Showdown. Every hand feeds four jackpots, Pair, Full House, Quads and Royal Flush. At random the cards come out and the wheel decides your hand. Jackpots are shown at your current bet.

Hyrox Incident. Roughly 1 hand in 155, something goes badly wrong at the Hyrox. It costs an extra 5× to 25× your bet on top of the stake (100 to 500 at a 20 bet). This is what pays for All Aboard and the Pearl Earring showing up so often.

The Rewatch. Any losing hand can be sent back for a rewatch, since Rob hasn't seen it anyway. Free re-spin, roughly 1 hand in 38.

Symbols, cheapest to best. Jack of spades, ace of diamonds, Rob Complains, Rob Gains, Rob Devouring His Son, Rob With the Pearl Earring, Rob Danes, and Rob Reigns on top.

ATM. Tap ATM any time you're not mid-spin, or bust and the Bank of Rob ATM opens. Crack the 4-digit PIN (no repeated digits) in 6 tries. Green means the right digit in the right spot, amber means right digit wrong spot, grey means not in the PIN. Crack it in 1 try for 50,000, down to 10,000 on try 6. Fail and the card is retained, so try another card.

The maths. Simulated over millions of hands, the machine returns about 92.5% of stakes, so the house still wins. About 1 hand in 3 pays something. Chips are play money only.
